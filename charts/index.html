How these numbers were made
Texts
One core rule text per religion, in public-domain English translations from 1611 to 1930. Hadith, Talmud and later legal traditions are not included.
Rule
A unit that commands, forbids, permits or penalizes a behaviour. Narrative, prophecy, prayers and one character's personal orders were excluded by hand.
Coding
Every gender-relevant rule was read and coded by one coder. Buddhist rules were coded from the structure of the monks' and nuns' codes, after a 20-rule spot-check.
Reliability
A blind re-code of 100 rules matched 100%, but the same coder made both passes, so this shows consistency, not independent agreement.
Not counted
Rules that name no gender. In the Bible, Torah and Quran these are most rules, and they are written with "he" as the default.
